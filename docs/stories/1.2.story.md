# Story 1.2: Consolidate the two Ultras races

## Status

Ready for Review

## Story

As a Kartódromo operator, I need the Ultras stage to read both LapTime races, keep Ultras Insanos and Ultras Rápidos in their UDK categories, and publish one combined classification with the two race scores summed per pilot.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. The stage is bound to exactly two distinct UDK sessions and LapTime races: normal track and inverted track.
2. LapTime is the source of the race state, position, laps, times, status, and penalties; the application does not recalculate timing penalties.
3. The configured race table gives the winner 50 base points in each race and the combined result sums race 1 and race 2 by UDK pilot identity, independently of kart number.
4. Only the active UDK categories Ultras Insanos and Ultras Rápidos are included in the stage classification.
5. A live snapshot is persisted in UDK and can be read publicly without exposing service credentials; the public display refreshes while either race is live and shows both race scores and the combined total.
6. The stage service exposes health and configuration diagnostics and has a restartable Windows startup wrapper.
7. LapTime, UDK sessions/categories/drivers, Supabase persistence, and the unresolved identity list are verified before the result is considered complete.
8. Lint, typecheck, tests, and production build pass.
